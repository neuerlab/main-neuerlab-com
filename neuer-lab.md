## Nicola Neuer

Nicola Neuer is a certified Neuro-Mental Coach, evidential medium, and author of The Longevity Guide. With over 10 years of practice she works at the intersection of neuroscience, mindfulness, spiritual practice, and evidential mediumship. Retreats in Bali and worldwide.

## The Longevity Guide

- [The Longevity Guide in English](https://www.neuerlab.com/longevity-guide): A 186-page workbook weaving neuroscience, nourishment, movement and spirit into a living practice. 7 chapters, 43 mindfulness practices, 54 reflection prompts, 30-day beginning. By Nicola Neuer. Launch price EUR 29.
- [Der Longevity Guide auf Deutsch](https://www.neuerlab.com/longevity-guide-de): The same workbook in German. 186 pages, 7 chapters, 43 practices, 54 reflection prompts. For Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

## Services

- [Mental Training](https://www.neuerlab.com/mental-training): 1:1 private sessions, 60 minutes, online or in person. Dissolve limiting beliefs, clarify values, solution-oriented exercises. Free 15-minute introductory call.
- [Mediumship and Psychic Reading](https://www.neuerlab.com/mediumship): Evidential mediumship sessions connecting with loved ones who have passed. Psychic readings for clarity on decisions, situations and direction. Online or in person.
- [Emotional Release](https://www.neuerlab.com/emotional-release): Family constellation work, values clarification, and affirmation practice. In person only, in Vienna, Austria, or Nicola travels to you.
- [Leadership and Awareness Workshop](https://www.neuerlab.com/#offerings): Full-day workshops for leadership teams. Belief systems, values, conscious communication, and purposeful goal-setting. Bespoke to each organisation.
