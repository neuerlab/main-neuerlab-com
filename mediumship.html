Mediumship & Psychic Reading

Evidence. Love. The connection that does not end.

Some things cannot be spoken about easily. The loss of someone. An unanswered question. A situation that does not resolve. This work creates a held, evidence-based space — for conversation, clarity, and the quiet comfort of knowing the connection is unbroken.

What Happens

What happens in a Mediumship Session

In a mediumship session, Nicola opens an energetic space and calls in the energy of those who have passed. What comes through is evidential — personality traits, physical appearance, shared memories, specific situations, and messages that carry the unmistakable signature of the person you knew.

You may ask questions. You may simply receive. Many people come seeking closure, peace, or simply the presence of someone they love. What arrives is often more specific — and more tender — than expected.

Psychic Reading

What happens in a Psychic Reading

Beyond mediumship, Nicola works with psychic perception — accessing information from your higher self and the field around you. This includes clarity on decisions, situations, relationships, and the direction you are considering. She can also read a person from a photograph — their character, core themes, and underlying dynamics — without prior information.

What to Bring

What you may bring to a session

  • A question about someone you have lost
  • A decision you cannot see clearly
  • A situation that needs resolution
  • A relationship you want to understand more deeply
  • A photograph of someone you want read
  • Simply the desire to feel the connection
Integrity

What this is. What this is not.

This work is evidence-based and deeply human. It is not performance, entertainment, or prediction. It is a space of genuine inquiry — held with care, conducted with integrity, and grounded in love.

Questions

What you may want to know.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No preparation is necessary. Come as you are. If you have a specific person in mind or a question, you may hold that quietly — but you do not need to share it in advance.

Can a session be done online?

Yes. Energy does not require physical proximity. Sessions are conducted online via video call with the same depth as in-person work.

What is the difference between mediumship and a psychic reading?

Mediumship connects with the energy of those who have passed. A psychic reading accesses information about living situations, people, and decisions from your higher self and the field around you. Both can be part of a single session.

How long is a session?

Sessions are 60 minutes.
